About this practice

This guided relaxation invites you to slow down, breathe a little deeper, and gently release the tension you've been holding. There's nothing to get right. Simply listen and let your body rest.

Before you begin

  • Find a quiet, comfortable place to sit or lie down.
  • Headphones help you settle in.
  • Give yourself a few minutes afterward before getting up.

Please don't listen while driving or doing anything that needs your full attention. This practice supports your wellbeing and is not a substitute for medical or mental health care.
