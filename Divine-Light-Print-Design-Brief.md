## 2. Brand look and feel
- **Mood:** calm, soothing, spiritual but grounded, premium, lots of breathing room.
- **Style:** soft misty lavender, generous whitespace, thin hairline borders, rounded corners, elegant serif headlines.
- **Avoid:** neon or saturated colors, busy backgrounds, heavy gradients, emoji, stock "spa" clichés, hard-sell language ("LIMITED TIME!!", "only 3 spots left"), any medical or cure claims.

### Logo
- Main logo: a pink **lotus** inside a golden sun halo with rays, flanked by two **peacock feathers**, between two **bamboo flutes** (with small red tassels), above teal water ripples.
- Wordmark: **DIVINE LIGHT** (all caps, wide letter spacing) with **"Healing and Life Coaching"** in italic underneath, set off by a thin line.
- Small spaces (stickers, corners, QR labels): use the **lotus icon** only.
- Keep the logo on light lavender, white or the dark plum. Never stretch it or recolor the lotus.

## 7. Print notes
- Screen colors (hex) look slightly different in print. Ask the printer for a **CMYK proof**; lavender can print grey if the printer shifts it, so check the proof before a full run.
- Use **matte or soft-touch** paper or vinyl for a calm, premium feel (avoid high gloss).
- Check every phone number, price and URL before printing.
- Disclaimer for any piece that lists Anxiety Support or healing services (small print is fine): "Complementary wellness practices; not a substitute for medical or mental-health care."
