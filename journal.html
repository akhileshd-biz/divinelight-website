Personal story

The path that chose me

My journey into spirituality began when I was in high school — a quiet nudge from within that led me to seek deeper truths. Over the past 25+ years, I've devoted myself to learning the art of working with energy, and to helping others find their own clarity along the way.

What began as a personal search led me to formal certification as a Reiki Master, and years later, further training as a Certified Life Coach through the Jay Shetty Certification School. Each step has deepened the same calling — helping people reconnect with their own inner power and move through change with more clarity and less fear.
